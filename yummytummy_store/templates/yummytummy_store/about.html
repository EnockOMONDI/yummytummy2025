Live Great Limited

Climate-Smart Agribusiness for Health & Sustainability

We are not just a food business – we are a climate-smart agribusiness that integrates nutrition, environmental sustainability, and community impact through innovative peanut value addition.

Mission

To improve nutrition, create sustainable livelihoods, and promote climate-resilient food systems by innovating around peanuts and other indigenous crops.

Vision

A world where communities thrive on nutritious, affordable, and climate-friendly foods, with agribusiness serving as a driver of health, sustainability, and prosperity.

What we do

Driving change through innovation and sustainability

Core Activities

  • Production of natural peanut butter, roasted peanuts, and nut-based blends
  • Peanut butter production Trainning
  • Bee keeping.
  • Distribution and sales
  • CSR, Promoting Climate literacy , waste management and circular economy.

Community Engagement & CSR

  • Climate literacy programs using GCA Toolkit in primary schools
  • Storytelling initiative (Kadogo Climate hero storybook)
  • Waste management projects – converting glass & plastic into eco-blocks
  • Sports & nutrition programs to promote health in the society

Distribution & Sales

  • Direct sales (physical & online via our ecommerce and social media)
  • Bulk supplies to 5 restaurants, banks, and 20 mini marts
  • Participation in expos and community events
