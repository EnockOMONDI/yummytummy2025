Owner Operations Guide

Start at Business Overview. Use Quick Actions to open the records you need. Access to the owner dashboard requires an active staff account with the owner-dashboard permission; superusers have access automatically.

Reading Business Overview

  • Captured payments includes successful, partially refunded and fully refunded payments. The selected period uses the capture completion date.
  • Successful refunds uses refund completion dates. Net collections subtracts refunds completed in the selected period from captures in that period. It is not profit and can be negative.
  • Legacy payments or refunds without completion dates appear in Needs Attention. They are included in All time, but not dated periods. Do not invent dates to make totals match.
  • Orders counts orders created in the selected period. Paid orders includes those subsequently refunded. Neither means delivered.
  • Products includes available and unavailable products. Recipes includes published and unpublished recipes. These are current catalog totals.
  • Registered customer accounts excludes staff and guest buyers; it does not measure all unique customers.
  • Fulfillment uses the latest physical-delivery event, independently of payment events. Digital-only orders are excluded.
  • Product and recipe sales show gross item values before order-level discounts and refunds. M-Pesa success rate uses finished attempts initiated in the selected period, not website conversion or provider uptime.

Dates use Africa/Nairobi. Custom end dates include the entire day. Apply / Refresh updates the view; the dashboard does not reload while you are reading it.

Products and Recipes

Choose Products to search, add or edit products. Manage sizes, additional prices and variant stock inside the product form. Product actions control availability and featured status. Stock alerts use a threshold of five units or fewer and distinguish base stock from variant stock.

Choose Recipes for descriptions, ingredient rows, preparation steps, pricing, images, PDFs and publishing. Rich-text content is sanitized. Missing-media alerts check whether media is configured; they do not prove that a remote image or file is reachable.

Recipe order lines retain the purchased title and price. Recipe purchase records represent digital access, which is granted after confirmed payment for an associated account.

Orders and Fulfillment

Orders opens the order list. Open an order to inspect its customer, delivery address, item snapshots, payment records and tracking history. Customer and financial snapshots are read-only here.

Use the order actions to progress through Processing, Packaging, Shipped, Out for Delivery and Delivered. Review the confirmation screen before applying changes. Backward moves, cancelled orders, digital-only orders and unpaid non-cash-on-delivery orders are skipped. Tracking history is append-only.

Payment and fulfillment are separate: receiving payment does not prove delivery, and delivery does not prove cash was collected. No rider assignment or live location tracking is implemented.

Payments and Refunds

M-Pesa callbacks correlate payment attempts and record provider events. Failed-payment customers can use a signed retry link to retry the original order. Do not manually mark M-Pesa payments successful.

For current pending or processing offline/cash-on-delivery payments, verify receipt externally and use Payments → Confirm selected offline payments as received. Review the confirmation screen. Refunded, cancelled, failed and superseded payments are excluded.

Refund records document refunds; saving one does not transfer money through M-Pesa. Confirm the external refund first. Only a superuser can approve a refund in this interface, and M-Pesa approval requires a provider reference. Finance staff cannot alter approved amounts or reasons. Successful refunds update the payment's refunded balance.

Guest Checkout and Manual Sales

Guests can shop without automatic account creation. No temporary passwords are generated or emailed. Email sign-in uses short-lived links. Guests use Track Order with the required order/contact details; account holders can view their order history.

Offline Orders opens the sales workspace. Create Manual Order records a sale using current database prices and stock. Users need add-order and view-product permissions. Recording an order does not confirm payment.

Notifications and Exceptions

Needs Attention links to matching records: failed/pending/processing payments, requested refunds, low stock, missing media, undated financial events and notification backlog. Record ages are shown without assuming an overdue delivery promise.

Notifications records queued, processing, sent and failed messages. Queued or failed messages can be retried by authorized staff. Processing messages older than 15 minutes are shown as stalled candidates. The process_notifications management command handles pending and stale jobs; production scheduling must be configured separately.

Sent means the email backend accepted the message, not proof the customer read or received it. Retries can duplicate messages if delivery succeeded before recording the result. Provider Events and Payment Attempts are audit records, not buttons for issuing payments.

Access and Roles

Owners can assign the Can view owner business dashboard permission through Users or Roles. This grants access to business summaries, including customer/order details, but does not automatically grant record-editing permissions. Quick Actions and record links follow each account's model permissions.

Use Sales, Catalog, Fulfillment, Finance, Content, Marketing and Support roles for their operational tasks. Keep superuser access limited to trusted administrators. Legacy automatic-account records are retained for history, not the default checkout journey.
