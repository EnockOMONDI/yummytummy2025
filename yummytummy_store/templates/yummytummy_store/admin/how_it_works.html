Session-Based Cart

Shopping cart data is stored in user sessions, allowing guests to shop without creating accounts. Cart persists across page visits and automatically converts to orders during checkout.

Auto Account Creation

Guest customers automatically get user accounts created during checkout. They receive email credentials and can track orders without manual registration.

M-Pesa Integration

Seamless mobile money payments through Safaricom M-Pesa STK Push. Real-time payment confirmation and automatic order status updates.

Product Variants

Products support multiple variants (sizes, weights) with different pricing. Customers can select variants through expandable product cards.

Order Tracking

Complete order lifecycle management from placement to delivery. Customers receive email updates and can track progress through their dashboard.

Coupon System

Flexible discount system with percentage and fixed amount coupons. Usage tracking and expiration date management included.

M-Pesa Payment System

How your store processes secure mobile money payments from Kenyan customers

How M-Pesa Payments Work

1

Payment Request

What Happens: Customer selects M-Pesa and enters their phone number
Behind the Scenes: Your store connects securely to Safaricom's payment system
Business Benefit: Automated payment processing reduces manual work

2

STK Push Notification

What Happens: Customer receives a payment prompt on their phone
Behind the Scenes: Safaricom sends a secure payment request with order details
Business Benefit: Familiar payment experience increases customer trust

3

Customer Authorization

What Happens: Customer enters their M-Pesa PIN to authorize payment
Behind the Scenes: Safaricom processes the payment securely
Business Benefit: Secure payment method reduces fraud risk

4

Payment Confirmation & Account Creation

What Happens: Order status updates to "Confirmed", customer account created, welcome email sent
Behind the Scenes: System receives payment confirmation and triggers automated processes
Business Benefit: Immediate order processing and customer onboarding

5

Error Handling

What Happens: If payment fails, order is marked as cancelled with clear reason
Behind the Scenes: System tracks all payment attempts and failures for support
Business Benefit: Clear error tracking helps resolve customer issues quickly
